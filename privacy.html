Privacy policy

Last updated: 13-11-2026.

Who is responsible

Eric Muñiz Genebriera, individual developer based in Spain. Contact: ericmunizgeneb@gmail.com.

Banking data: processed only in your Odoo

Bank certificates, payment files, IBANs and account-holder names are processed only in your own Odoo database. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see them.

What is stored, and where

There are no ORIVEXY accounts: you use the module with your Odoo user. Everything the module stores (validations, uploaded files, results and, with the add-on, certificates linked to bank accounts) stays in your own Odoo database; we have no access to it. Purchases on Odoo Apps are handled by Odoo S.A. under its own privacy policy.

Internet connections

The Odoo module makes no external calls.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to the email above. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
