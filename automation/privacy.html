ORIVEXY Automation

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

ORIVEXY Automation ("the app") is a desktop application for Windows that helps a creator produce videos and manage their own YouTube channel. The app runs entirely on the user's computer. There is no ORIVEXY server: we do not receive, store or have access to any of your data.

Google user data the app accesses

When you connect your channel, you sign in with Google and grant these permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the videos you create or choose to your channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to show your channel profile, your videos and their public statistics inside the app;
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — to show your channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, countries, devices, demographics and estimated revenue) inside the app.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's features to you: uploading your videos, editing their title, description or visibility when you ask, and displaying your channel information and analytics. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties and not used to train AI models.

Storage and security

  • The OAuth token is stored only on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Channel data and analytics are cached in a local database on your computer so the app can show charts.
  • Nothing is transmitted anywhere except directly between your computer and Google's official APIs.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app or deleting its data folder removes every locally stored copy of your data.

Limited Use

ORIVEXY Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
