ORIVEXY Automation

A desktop app for Windows that runs locally on your computer.

  • Creates short explainer videos on your PC with local AI models.
  • Uploads them to your own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Shows your channel profile, videos and YouTube Analytics in one place.

Your Google data never leaves your computer except to talk directly to Google's APIs. Read the Privacy Policy.
