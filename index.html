What it does

Before you send a payment batch, ORIVEXY Verify looks up every IBAN in the bank's ownership certificate and compares the holder's name. It tells you which payments are correct, which go to someone else and which are missing.

Who it is for. Anyone who pays suppliers from Odoo or prepares SEPA batches: accounts, treasury, administration. Also for accounting firms that check their clients' batches with the desktop app, without Odoo.

What it checks. That the IBAN is valid (85 countries, length and check digits), that it is in the certificate and that the holder matches. Accents, capitals and "S.L." versus "SL" do not count; a different surname does. It also warns when an IBAN repeats in the batch.

Where. Inside your Odoo, on your server or Odoo.sh, or on your computer with the app. IBANs, names and files never leave your installation: the module calls no external service.

How to start. Try the demo in your browser, with fictitious data and no sign-up. Then buy the module on Odoo Apps for your Odoo version, or ask for the desktop app by email.

Simplified rules for this page. The demo runs the full engine.

Type the holder as you have it.

Capitals, accents, commas and "S.L." versus "SL" do not count. Word order only stops mattering when the words are exactly the same.

Change the legal form.

S.L. versus S.A. or GmbH is not a detail: it may be another company. It goes to manual review, never to correct.

Get one digit wrong.

A typo breaks the IBAN check digits and is caught before the certificate is even looked at.

Remove the certificate.

If the IBAN is in no certificate, it is not taken as good: ask for the certificate or confirm the account.

  1. Copy the orivexy_validator folder into your addons.
  2. Install the libraries: pip install -r orivexy_validator/requirements.txt
  3. Turn on developer mode, update the apps list, search "ORIVEXY" and press Activate.

Scanned PDFs need Tesseract OCR on the server. Odoo.sh does not include it: use the text PDF, the SEPA XML or the app.

Questions

Does it replace my bank's Verification of Payee?

No, it complements it. Since 9 October 2025 euro-area banks must check name and IBAN on euro transfers, but for batch payments a business may opt out of that check. ORIVEXY checks the whole batch before you send it, against the ownership certificate issued by your supplier's bank, and keeps a record of what was checked and who decided.

What if a name is written differently?

Accents, capitals, punctuation and "S.L." versus "SL" do not count. Word order is ignored only when exactly the same words appear. A different or missing surname is flagged, and doubtful cases go to manual review.

Does it ask the bank or block payments?

No. It compares your batch with the certificate you provide. It does not query the bank, does not prove that an account exists or is active, nor that the certificate is genuine. The verification module does not block payments: it tells you which ones to review.

Which files does it accept?

Certificates: text PDF, scanned PDF (with OCR) and SEPA XML. Batches: TXT, CSV, TSV, DAT and SEPA XML pain.001 or pain.008. PDFs up to 64 MB; batches and XML up to 24 MB. In Odoo, results export to PDF, CSV and Excel with IBANs masked; the desktop app exports CSV and Excel.

Does it work on Odoo Online?

No, because it needs its own Python libraries. It works on Odoo 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 20, Community and Enterprise, on your server or on Odoo.sh.

Does my data leave the company?

No. Everything is processed on your Odoo server or your computer; the module makes no external calls. The desktop app only connects to activate or check the licence (your email) and to look for updates on GitHub; and, only if you link it, to your own Odoo. It never sends IBANs, names or files to ORIVEXY.

Which languages?

The module and the desktop app are in English, Spanish and Catalan; the module's result notes are in Spanish. It reads certificates in 9 languages and validates IBANs of the 85 countries that use them.

How do I know it gets it right?

Version 0.1.13 was measured on 2,000 synthetic pairs of look-alike names: no false "correct". The published version passes more than 750 automated tests. Even so, always confirm before you pay.
