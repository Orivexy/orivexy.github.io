What the product does — and does not do

ORIVEXY Verify compares the IBANs and account holders of your payment file with the bank ownership certificates you provide. It does not query any bank, does not prove that an account exists or is active, does not prove that a certificate is authentic and does not block payments. Results are an aid: you remain responsible for checking payments before making them.
